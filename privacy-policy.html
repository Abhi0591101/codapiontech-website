Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us

We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring that no user content is published, shared, or displayed publicly without your clear consent.

What this means

Codapion Technologies Pvt. Ltd respects the privacy of every visitor and client. Any information you share through our website, contact form, or email is used only to respond to your inquiry or provide requested services.

  • No user content is published on public pages, social media, or any other public channel.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Any submitted content remains confidential and is handled only for the purpose intended by you.
  • If you have questions about privacy or data handling, please contact us directly.

If you choose to contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and message details solely to respond to your request. This information is never published or used for unrelated purposes.
